About Me

I am an MSc candidate in Neuroscience at McMaster University, supervised by Dr. Nicholas Bock, investigating the relationship between intracortical myelin and major depressive disorder. My work centers on quantitative neuroimaging (qMRI), computational analysis pipelines, and translating neuroimaging data to improve diagnostic and therapeutic frameworks for neuropsychiatric disorders.

Bridging quantitative research with interdisciplinary perspectives, I aim to understand the structural substrates of mental health and contribute meaningful insights to neuropsychiatric research.
