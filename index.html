About Me

My name is Nicolas Viulet, and I am currently pursuing my Master of Science at McMaster University, where I'm investigating the relationship between intracortical myelin and depression. I'm passionate about understanding how the brain works and how we can leverage neuroimaging data to improve mental health outcomes, develop new diagnostic protocols, and improve therapeutic approaches for neuropsychiatric disorders. I hope to develop my computational and clinical skills in neuroscience by working at the intersection of scientific research and clinical applications.

Beyond my current research, my background in Buddhism, Psychology, and Mental Health has shaped how I think about neuroscience—not just as a technical discipline, but as a way to understand the complexity human experience. I believe in the importance of interdisciplinary collaboration and integrating mindfulness into my academic and personal life.
